On/off toggle (`checked` / `defaultChecked` / `onCheckedChange`, `size`: `default` | `sm`). Pair with a `Label`; used for enabling streamers and notification settings.

## Example

```jsx
<div className="flex items-center gap-2"><Switch id="n" defaultChecked /><Label htmlFor="n">Benachrichtigungen aktiv</Label></div>
```
