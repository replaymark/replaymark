Hairline divider (`orientation="horizontal" | "vertical"`) in the rule colour. Replaymark prefers hairlines over shadows to separate content.

## Example

```jsx
<div className="flex h-5 items-center gap-3 text-sm">
  <span>Übersicht</span><Separator orientation="vertical" /><span>Timeline</span>
</div>
```
