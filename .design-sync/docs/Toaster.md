Sonner toast host; mount once near the root (`position="bottom-right"` in the app) and trigger toasts with `window.Replaymark.toast('…')` (also `toast.success`, `toast.error` – sonner's API). Styled with popover tokens; follows the theme from `ReplaymarkProvider`.

## Example

```jsx
<Toaster position="bottom-right" />
```
